1  What the module is

HR Modules groups the ten screens that act on other people rather than on yourself. Unlike My Team, these are not scoped to a reporting line — they are company-wide, and access is controlled purely by permission.

ScreenPurpose
User ManagementCreate and maintain user accounts
Role ManagementDefine roles and what each may do
HR AnnouncementsPublish company notices
Leave CreditsGrant and correct leave balances
Employee RegularizationMove staff from probationary to regular
HolidaysMaintain the holiday calendar
Associate LogsFile documents against an associate
Active AssociatesSee who is clocked in right now
Proxy LeavesFile a leave on someone's behalf

2  User Management

The account register. A user row carries name, email, status, role, team, shift and immediate supervisor — that last field is what populates every My Team screen, so getting it right matters beyond this page.

  • Create, edit, view and soft-delete; a bulk action endpoint handles multi-row changes.
  • A password is optional at creation — sign-in can be done by emailed magic link.
  • Deleting is a soft delete, so history and audit trails survive.
Email validation needs live DNS. The rule is email:rfc,dns, which requires the domain to resolve. Addresses on a non-resolving domain such as .test are rejected through the UI even though seeders create them happily.

3  Role Management

Roles and permissions, built on Spatie's permission package. The screen lists roles with a permission count and opens a modal grouping every permission by module, with per-module and global select-all.

Two guards, always in step. Every role and permission exists twice, once for web and once for sanctum. The API authenticates with sanctum, so a permission created only for web appears in the UI but denies every request.

Roles ship seeded: Super Admin (everything), Admin, HR, Manager, Team Lead and Employee. RolePermissionSeeder is the single source of truth and uses syncPermissions, which replaces a role's set rather than adding to it.

4  HR Announcements

Image-based company notices. Because the content is a picture, this screen is a gallery rather than a table: a card grid with the image, title, date and author, plus a lightbox for the full-size view.

  • Upload accepts JPG, PNG, GIF and WebP up to 5 MB.
  • A title is optional; left blank it becomes “Company Update”.
  • Recent active announcements also surface on the Dashboard.

5  Leave Credits

Where balances are granted and corrected. One row per user per year, holding VL, SL, PTO, CTO and birthday leave.

ActionEffect
EditSet credits, used and pending for one user
ImportBulk-load balances from a file
ExportDownload the current balances
Reset pendingClear a user's pending buckets when they have drifted
Pending is normally automatic. LeaveObserver moves days between pending and used as leaves are filed, approved, rejected and cancelled. Reset pending is a repair tool for when direct database edits have left the arithmetic out of step — not part of the normal flow.

CTO is the one bucket measured in hours rather than days. It is this company's compensatory-time concept and is granted here by HR; there is no overtime request to file.

6  Employee Regularization

Lists probationary staff with their hire date and the regularization date due, and lets HR set or clear that date. Setting it marks the employee Regular.

That status is not cosmetic — it decides the leave scheme:

Employment statusLeave treatment
RegularFull annual grant up front (“Fixed”)
Anything elseAccrues monthly (“Accrual”)

The same rule is shown on Profile and applied by LeaveCreditSeeder.

7  Holidays

The holiday calendar, filterable by month and calendar.

TypeLabel
regularRegular Holiday
special_non_workingSpecial (Non-Working)
companyCompany Holiday

A calendar field allows more than one set — useful where a client site observes different days. Holidays can be deactivated rather than deleted (is_active), which keeps history intact.

Viewing is not managing. view holidays is held by every role so the calendar is visible to all; create, edit and delete are HR-side only.

8  Associate Logs

A document trail against an individual — a memo, a notice, an acknowledgement. Each entry carries a date, the detail text, the author and an optional file.

An associate can download their own filed documents from Profile, through a route scoped to the signed-in user, so one person cannot reach another's file by guessing an ID.

9  Active Associates

A live view of who is clocked in, auto-refreshing every 30 seconds. Three tiles count Working, On Break and Total Active; the table lists each person with status, time in and department.

Search here is client-side — it filters the rows already loaded rather than re-querying, which keeps typing instant. The tiles deliberately show the unfiltered headcount.

10  Proxy Leaves and Proxy Attendance

Two screens for acting on behalf of someone who cannot. Proxy Leaves files a leave against another user; Proxy Attendance records or corrects an attendance row.

  • Both offer cut-off period helpers, so entries can be placed in the right payroll window.
  • Both expose stats and CSV export alongside the list.
  • A proxy-filed leave is an ordinary leave once created — LeaveObserver moves credits exactly as it would for a self-filed one.
Separately permissioned. Proxy create, edit and delete are distinct permissions from viewing, so an auditor can be given sight of proxy activity without the ability to file.

12  Permissions

AreaHeld by
Users, roles, announcements, associate logs, active associatesSuper Admin, Admin; HR has all but role management and user deletion
Leave credits, employee regularization, holidays, proxy screensSuper Admin, Admin, HR
view holidays aloneAll six roles

Manager, Team Lead and Employee hold none of the HR-side permissions. They reach the same underlying data only through My Records and My Team, which are scoped to themselves and their direct reports.
